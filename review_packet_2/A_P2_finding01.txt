P2 — FINDING_01 closed: term_structure_slope populated, v3 -> v3.1
==================================================================

THE DEFECT
----------
app/shortduration/scoring/components.py:137 has read
`IVContext.term_structure_slope` since v3, applying a 0.85 multiplier to the
volatility component when the structure is backwardated (front IV richer than
back = IV-crush risk for a debit buyer).

No live provider ever populated the field. The mock provider did.

Consequence: the penalty fired in every test run and had never once fired in
production. The tested model and the shipped model were different models, for
the entire life of v3. This is the canonical instance of "the freeze is about
BEHAVIOUR, not about which files you edited" — the fix touches no file under
app/shortduration/scoring/ and is nonetheless a model change.

-------

  test_a_null_term_slope_scores_the_same_as_a_flat_one pins the deadband
  behaviour explicitly; test_an_empty_or_malformed_response_yields_none_not_zero
  pins the provider side.

WHY IT WAS FIXED BEFORE THE WINDOW RATHER THAN DEFERRED (Ruling 1)
-------------------------------------------------------------------
Zero signals had been captured. There was no corpus to split, so no result could
be contaminated by the change. Deferring would have meant opening the window on a
model known to differ from the tested one.
