HONEST NOTE ON THE `skipped` ABOVE. freeze-guard is `if: github.event_name ==
'pull_request'`. It has no base..head range to diff on a push, so it cannot run
there and is skipped rather than passing vacuously. The guard's protection is
therefore on the PR, which is where changes are actually reviewed and where
branch protection can require it. A direct push to main bypasses it — that is a
real limitation of the control, stated rather than papered over. Closing it
requires branch protection making `freeze-guard` a required status check (P9,
NOT_STARTED — see A_P9).
