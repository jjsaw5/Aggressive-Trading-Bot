P5 — Data dictionary covers every exported column
=================================================
audit_export/signals_data_dictionary.md — 313 lines, 90 documented column
entries covering the 86 columns the exporter emits (the surplus entries are the
sentinel-vocabulary and provenance rows, documented in the same table format).

Rewritten this session: sections 1C and 1E were previously placeholder headings.
Committed 4108f62 (PR #51).

Every column carries, at minimum: what it is, its unit, whether it is MEASURED
or MODELED, and which sentinel appears when it is absent.

THE AWKWARD ONES — the entries that exist because the number can mislead:

  mfe_per_share / mae_per_share
    Documented as BOUNDS, not achieved prices. A minute bar's high and low have
    no ordering within the bar, so the structure's best and worst values need
    not have been simultaneously available. Naming them "best fill achieved"
    would be a fabrication.

  mark_coverage_pct / max_gap_minutes / grade_confidence
    Documented as travelling WITH the grade, not as metadata. An exit that
    triggered and reversed inside a gap is MISSED, not mispriced — so the error
    is DIRECTIONAL: trades look longer-held than they were and stop-outs are
    under-reported. The dictionary says this in those words.

  greeks_source / cost_stress_source / iv_rank_source / pop_source / chain_source
    Every modeled quantity names its derivation. Greeks are Black-Scholes
    because no provider supplies them. cost_stress_source distinguishes
    "effective_from_side_volume" (derived from executions) from "nbbo" (a real
    quoted book) — never blank when a stress figure is present, because the two
    are not interchangeable.

  entry_spot
    Documented as nullable, with the reason: it was a required float, builders
    wrote `or 0.0`, and 67 of 67 audited scanner rows reported a spot of zero.

  NA_not_implemented vs NA_no_data vs NA_unresolved
    The distinction is load-bearing and is spelled out: the first means no such
    concept exists for this row, the second means the concept exists and this
    row lacks it, the third means resolution was attempted and did not conclude.
    Collapsing the first two would hide which gaps are buildable.

VERIFICATION
------------
Coverage was checked programmatically against the exporter's own column list
rather than by reading — the check is that no column the exporter emits is
absent from the dictionary, and it passes.
