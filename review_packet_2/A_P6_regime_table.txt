NO LOOKAHEAD
------------
`regime_as_of(rows, when)` returns the most recent session STRICTLY BEFORE
`when` (app/analytics/daily_regime.py:148, docstring at line 11). A signal
generated on session D is grouped by the regime known at the close of D-1. Using
D's own close would leak the session's outcome into its own regime label.

EXISTING ROWS ARE NEVER REWRITTEN
----------------------------------
build_regime_table.py leaves existing sessions alone. A regime row describes a
closed session and never legitimately changes; a vendor revision is a decision
for a human, not a silent rewrite of rows other analyses have already grouped by.

WHAT THE TABLE IMMEDIATELY SHOWED
----------------------------------
The audited scanner sessions clustered into effectively one regime
(highvol_below, highvol_below, midvol_below, lowvol_above across 07-28..07-31,
with the two highvol_below sessions carrying the bulk of the signals). That is
the quantitative confirmation of what the audit could only assert: the corpus
could not distinguish a bad model from a bad week. It is also the reason the
capture window needs a regime spread before any per-regime claim is made.
