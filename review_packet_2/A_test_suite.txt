STATUS LINE ON THAT ERROR. CI lints `app/ scripts/ tests/` and does not lint
`alembic/`. The error is in a migration I wrote this session (P6), so my earlier
"ruff clean" claim was true of CI's scope and not of the repository. Fixed in
this session's commit (import sort only; no behaviour change; migrations are
outside the guarded scoring paths). Reported rather than quietly corrected
because the mismatch between "CI is green" and "the repo is clean" is itself
worth knowing: CI's lint scope has a hole in it, and alembic/ is in the hole.
