B5 — `confidence` is not an independent reading
==============================================================

The 1-5DTE board renders, per row:

    tradability <strong>NN</strong>/100  ·  confidence NN%  ·  data quality NN%
    (app/web/dashboard.html:1111-1112)

Three numbers, presented as three readings. They are two.

  app/shortduration/scoring/engine.py:108
      overall = round(normalized * (0.6 + 0.4 * data_quality), 4)

monotone transform of two numbers already on the same line. Reproduced below

Finding. Rank order by `confidence` is identical to rank order by `score`
wherever data_quality is constant, and near-identical otherwise. A reader
comparing "tradability 77" against "confidence 72%" believes a second,
corroborating instrument agrees with the first. There is no second instrument.

This is not a false number — the arithmetic is correct and `data_quality` is
real. It is a false IMPLICATION, produced by presentation: two derived
quantities laid out as peers. The honest rendering states the dependence, e.g.
"tradability 77/100 (data quality 83%)" with no separate confidence figure, or
labels it "score x data quality".

Severity: this is the one display element on the board that manufactures
corroboration. Everything else on the row understates rather than overstates.
